Our Story

Why We Built a Table for This

Debt doesn't happen because women are bad with money. It happens because life is expensive, the pay gap is real, and most financial advice was never written with our lives in mind. We built Debt Free Dinner Club to change that.

Who We're For

Debt Free Dinner Club is built for women carrying credit card balances, student loans, medical debt, or "how did this happen" debt — women who are tired, motivated, and ready for a real plan instead of another lecture. Maybe you're supporting a family on one income. Maybe you're starting over after a divorce, a layoff, or a medical bill that spiraled. Maybe you just want to stop feeling behind. Whatever brought you here, this table has a seat for you.

We're not a bank, a debt settlement company, or a credit repair service — and we'll never pressure you into one. We're a community and a free directory that points you toward honest information, real tools, and other women doing the same work.

What We Believe

🙅‍♀️

No Shame, Ever

However the debt happened, you don't owe anyone an explanation. Judgment doesn't pay off a single dollar — a plan does.

📣

Plain Language Only

No jargon, no acronyms without explanation, no advice that assumes you already have a finance degree.

🧭

Real Life, Real Plans

Your plan should fit your actual income and actual bills — not a spreadsheet built for someone else's life.

👯‍♀️

Community Over Willpower

Willpower runs out. A table full of women cheering you on doesn't. Support is a strategy, not a nice-to-have.

🔍

Honest Recommendations

We only point you toward tools, videos, and organizations we believe are genuinely useful — including nonprofit and free options first.

🎯

Progress Over Perfection

You don't need a perfect budget or a perfect month. You need the next right step — and then the one after that.
