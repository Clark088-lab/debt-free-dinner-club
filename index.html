What Is Debt Free Dinner Club?

Think of it as pulling up a seat at a table full of women who are all working toward the same goal: freedom from debt. We pair simple, judgment-free money coaching with a warm, supportive community of women — so you're never figuring it out alone. No shame, no lectures, no jargon. Just honest conversations and a step-by-step plan built around your real life, your real income, and your real goals.
