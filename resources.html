Free Tools & Calculators

No spreadsheet skills required — plug in your numbers and let these do the math.

Undebt.it

A free debt snowball and avalanche calculator that builds a month-by-month payoff plan and lets you compare strategies side by side.

Visit undebt.it →

PowerPay

A free, ad-free debt payoff planner built by Utah State University Extension — enter your debts and get a personalized elimination schedule.

Visit powerpay.org →

CFPB Budgeting Tools

The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau's free worksheets and tools for building a budget and understanding your debt-to-income ratio.

Visit consumerfinance.gov →

Nonprofit Credit Counseling (Real Help, Not a Sales Pitch)

If you need more than a calculator, start here before you consider any paid debt relief company.

National Foundation for Credit Counseling (NFCC)

The largest nonprofit network of certified credit counselors in the U.S. Free or low-cost sessions cover budgeting, debt management plans, and more.

Visit nfcc.org →

FTC: Choosing a Debt Relief Company

The Federal Trade Commission's plain-language guide to spotting debt relief and debt settlement scams before you sign anything.

Visit consumer.ftc.gov →

FindHelp.org

Search by ZIP code for free local assistance with bills, food, and financial counseling — helpful if debt is tied to a bigger financial squeeze.

Visit findhelp.org →

211

A free, confidential referral line and website connecting you to local financial assistance and counseling services across the U.S.

Visit 211.org →

Books Worth Reading

Written by and for women navigating money and debt — check your library before you buy.

Clever Girl Finance — Bola Sokunbi

A practical, encouraging guide to ditching debt, saving money, and building wealth, written specifically for women.

Broke Millennial — Erin Lowry

A no-shame, conversational walkthrough of the money conversations most of us never had growing up, including tackling debt.

Get Good With Money — Tiffany Aliche

The Budgetnista's 10-step plan to financial wholeness, with a full section dedicated to getting out of debt for good.

The Total Money Makeover — Dave Ramsey

The book behind the debt snowball method, if you want the original deep-dive into that strategy.
